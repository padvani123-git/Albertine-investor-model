Site

Location & Product

A wooded infill parcel off Mt. Albertine Way, surrounded by established San Diego neighborhoods — walkable to Mission Valley and minutes from Mission Bay.

Two Distinct Models

1 variation plus 2 reverse floorplans. Lot sizes range from 5,000 to 20,000 square feet.

Mid-Century Architecture

2,000–2,400 sq. ft., two stories, designed around indoor/outdoor living.

Ease of Build

Bold simplicity, flexible living, sunken living rooms — designed by Basile Studio and Jules Wilson Design Studio.
